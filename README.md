# Analyzing the Impact of Lifestyle and Demographic Factors on Obesity (BRFSS 2024)

---

## Executive Summary:
Using SQL and Power BI, I performed a descriptive analysis of the BRFSS 2024 dataset to explore the relationship between obesity and different lifestyle, demographic, and socioeconomic factors. The data was cleaned and transformed to ensure consistency and accuracy for analysis.

An interactive dashboard was developed to visualize patterns related to obesity across different population groups.

Through this analysis, I explored how factors such as physical activity, age, income, smoking, and chronic diseases are associated with obesity prevalence.

These insights help identify high-risk groups and better understand the factors that contribute to obesity in the population.

## Business Problem:
Obesity is a growing public health issue, but it is not fully clear how different lifestyle and demographic factors contribute to it.

Because of that, it is important to understand:

- How obesity is distributed across different age groups
- How lifestyle habits like physical activity and smoking are related to obesity
- How socioeconomic factors such as income affect obesity rates
- The relationship between obesity and chronic diseases

Without this analysis, it is difficult to identify high-risk groups and understand which factors are most strongly associated with obesity.

## Methodology:
For this project, I used SQL and Power BI to analyze the BRFSS 2024 dataset.

First, I cleaned and transformed the data using SQL to ensure it was consistent and ready for analysis.

Then in Power BI, I created measures and calculated fields to support key metrics and comparisons across different population groups.

I chose Power BI because it allows for clear visualization of patterns and relationships between variables in large datasets.

Finally, I built an interactive dashboard to explore obesity trends across demographic and lifestyle factors.

## Skills:
SQL: aggregate functions, joins, case.
Power BI: ETL, DAX, data visualization.

## Results and Insights:
From the analysis, several clear patterns were identified between obesity and lifestyle and demographic factors.

Obesity rates were higher among individuals with lower levels of physical activity. Age also showed a clear pattern, with obesity increasing in middle-aged groups and peaking around 50–54 years.

Income level also showed a relationship with obesity, where lower-income groups tended to have higher obesity prevalence. In addition, certain chronic health conditions such as diabetes and heart disease were more common among obese individuals.

## Business Recommendations:
Based on these findings, the following actions can be considered:

- Focus on public health programs that promote physical activity
- Target high-risk age groups with prevention and awareness campaigns
- Develop interventions for lower-income populations who show higher obesity rates
- Increase awareness about the link between obesity and chronic diseases
