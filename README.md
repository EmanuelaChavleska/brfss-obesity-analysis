# Analyzing the Impact of Lifestyle and Demographic Factors on Obesity (BRFSS 2024)

## 📌 Overview

The goal of this project is to analyze the relationship between obesity and various factors such as physical activity, smoking, demographic characteristics, and socioeconomic conditions. The analysis also explores how obesity is associated with different health conditions.

The data used in this project comes from the Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System (BRFSS 2024), a large public dataset containing information on health risk behaviors, chronic diseases, and preventive healthcare usage in the United States.

The analysis was performed using Power BI, focusing on data transformation, descriptive analysis, and interactive visualizations to identify patterns and high-risk population groups.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze relationship between obesity and lifestyle factors
- Identify high-risk population groups
- Explore connection between obesity and diseases

## 📊 Key Insights

- Obesity affects approximately one-third of the population
- Physical inactivity is associated with higher obesity rates
- Obesity increases with age, peaking at 50–54
- Lower income is linked to higher obesity prevalence
- Obesity is associated with multiple chronic diseases (diabetes, heart disease)
